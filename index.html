Meet the founders

We're Théa & Lorène.

We're two friends and surfers. We've been living abroad for years, surfing wherever the waves take us. Lorène has been a freelance developer and digital nomad for 5 years. Théa's path ran through finance in Barcelona, running a hostel in Sydney, and she's now a diving instructor in the Philippines. Two different journeys, one shared life: travel, meet people, surf.

Everywhere we go (Australia, Indonesia, the Canary Islands, the Philippines) it's the same story: we don't know which spot to hit, where to rent a board, or who to paddle out with. And after every session, a big debrief on WhatsApp that gets lost in the thread.

So we started building the app we'd always dreamed of: a place to find surf buddies, log your sessions, and explore the spots, cafés and locals that make every surf trip special.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Is Foamies free?

Yes! Foamies is free to join and free to use for every surfer.

Is this a dating app?

Only if you fall for a surf spot. We're here to find people to surf with, not to match.

What can I do with Foamies?

Find surf buddies near you, log your sessions, explore spots and find trusted local pros.

Do I need to know how to surf to join?

If you've swallowed seawater at least once, you're one of us.

Who's behind Foamies?

Two friends and surfers, Théa & Lorène, building it with the surf community.

Where do the spots and info come from?

From the community. Every surfer who shares a spot or a tip makes Foamies better for the next one. You included.

I run a surfcamp, a guesthouse, a shop… or I'm a coach, shaper or photographer?

Yes, there's a spot for you. The pro side is still in the works: request your listing now and be there from day one, in front of every surfer who joins the app.
